**CruitParser — A Recruitment Automation Platform**

an Application Tracking & Parser System
* Designed and developed an automated recruitment platform for processing high-volume applications, extracting CV data, and assigning unique Applicant IDs.
* Implemented multilingual email/CV processing for **English, French, and Portuguese**, with automated candidate data extraction and classification.
* Built configurable screening rules for **age, nationality, education, and years of experience**, automatically identifying qualified, probable, and disqualified candidates.
* Developed a **weighted scoring and ranking engine** that evaluates candidates against position-specific criteria and pre-screening questions.
* Implemented multi-level recruitment workflows, enabling Recruitment Assistants, Consultants, and Specialists to progressively screen, score, and rank candidates.
* Designed configurable parameters and scoring rules so recruitment teams can adapt the system to different job descriptions and hiring requirements.
Tech: React, Nodejs, Nest, Typescript,Javascript, Redis, Docker, Aws, AI
