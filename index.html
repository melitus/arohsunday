About Me

Aroh Sunday is a senior backend engineer based in Montreal, Quebec, Canada — known for production AI pipelines, distributed systems, and blockchain trust protocols. Projects include GameVoc Africa, CribMD, GreenAnt, and contributions to the XRPL Commons Magazine.

My work sits at the intersection of backend architecture, cloud infrastructure, and intelligent systems. I architect microservices, design event-driven pipelines, build AI-powered workflows, and develop smart contracts that solve real trust problems.

Currently at BusPas Inc. through Mitacs Accelerate, I build MAS-DQA — a multi-agent framework for verifiable data quality in mobility streams. As an SE4AI CREATE Fellow at Polytechnique Montréal, I engineer AI-based software systems for production environments.
